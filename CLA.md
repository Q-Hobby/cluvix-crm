## How to sign

You sign once; the signature covers all of Your future Contributions to the Project.

**Individuals (Part A)**: when you open your first pull request, post the following comment on
that pull request, exactly as written:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The comment, together with your GitHub account name and the time of the comment, is recorded
as your signature. This format is compatible with automated CLA tools (such as CLA Assistant),
which the Project may enable to record signatures and check them on each pull request.

**Entities (Part B)**: contact Cluvix Solutions as described in Part B before your Designated
Employees submit Contributions.

Pull requests from contributors who have not signed the CLA cannot be merged.
